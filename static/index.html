Special Rules

  • PEDro Scale (RCT only): Criteria C2-C11 scored out of 10. Base methodology score derived from PEDro ratio.
  • AMSTAR-2 (SR/MA only): 16 criteria assessed for systematic reviews and meta-analyses. 7 critical criteria (A2, A4, A7, A9, A11, A13, A15) are weighted 1.5× in the score calculation.
  • NOS (Cohort/XS only): Newcastle-Ottawa Scale — 9 items across 3 domains: Selection (4 stars), Comparability (2 stars), Outcome (3 stars). Score = stars earned / stars assessable, ratio × design cap.
  • GRADE (Consensus only): 8 items assess consensus process quality: systematic search, evidence grading, structured consensus method, panel composition, COI management, recommendation strength, evidence gaps, and external review.
  • Elite Athlete Exception: Small sample penalty waived when population is elite/professional (e.g., N=15 national team players).
  • COI Filter: Applied only when COI is obvious (funder undeniably sells the product tested). Ambiguous COI is flagged but not penalized.
  • BLACK FLAG: Reserved exclusively for predatory journals. COI does not trigger BLACK FLAG.
  • Null-on-Unknown: If data cannot be reliably extracted from the paper, it is left null — never invented.
  • Quality % (ring): Percentage of the maximum achievable score for that study design (e.g. 6.5/7 = 93%). Enables fair comparison across different study types. The ring color reflects this percentage: Excellent ≥ 85% Good 70–84% Fair 50–69% Poor 30–49% Critical < 30%
  • Confidence (extraction reliability): How many key fields the AI successfully extracted. High ≥ 80%  ·  Medium ≥ 55%  ·  Low < 55%. Low confidence signals a hard-to-read PDF or incomplete information in the paper.
